# Skyrim SE vanilla waterfall models: dissection

Source: `Skyrim - Meshes0.bsa` / `Skyrim - Meshes1.bsa` (BSA v105). The BSA path list is in `bsa_list.txt`: 187 paths match waterfall|fxwater|fxfall|splash|mist|foam|wake|rapids|cascade. 188 NIFs are extracted to `extracted/`, which also includes the falls-line and stream-rapids files. No `FXWaterfallSplash*`, `FXWaterfallMist*` (apart from MistBlast/MistBlastLite) or "riverfoam"/wake meshes exist in vanilla. Waterfall-related files are `FXWaterfallBody{Tall,Tall02,Slope}`, `FXWaterfallSkirt{Slope,SlopeNoMist,TallFront,TallFrontSlope}`, `FXWaterfallMistBlast{,Lite}`, `FXWaterfallThin*` (11), `lod/waterfalls/*_lod.nif`, `dlc01 FXWaterfallBodyTall02{NoLOD,MovLOD}`, `FXRapids*` and the Markarth/Whiterun waterfall pieces.

Tools (in `tools/`): `bsa.py` (BSA reader + LZ4 frame), `nifhdr.py`, and `nifxml.py`, a generic interpreter for the supplied nif.xml. Across all 188 extracted NIFs, every one of the 8970 blocks parsed and consumed exactly its declared block size. `analyze.py` and `summarize.py` produce this report.

## Conventions used in this report (verified)

-   **Matrices:** the 9 rotation floats are stored row-major for the engine's `v' = R*v + T` (i.e. the nif.xml-named `m11,m21,m31` triplet is ROW 0). I verified this on `wolf.nif`, where bone transform times skin bind transform gives identity translation with this order (error 0.0) and 121 units of error with the other order. "Model space" below means NIF root space with all node transforms applied.
-   **UV transform:** the shaders compute `uv' = uv * UVScale + UVOffset` (`Effect.hlsl` and `Lighting.hlsl`, `TexcoordOffset.zw/.xy`). Controllers on "U/V Offset" drive `UVOffset`. When the offset decreases at rate r per second, the texture pattern moves toward **+v** at `r / UVScale.v` mesh-v units per second.
-   **Quadratic keys:** every scroll controller has 2 keys, (0, 0) and (T, -1), with tangents of -1 per key span. The motion is linear, -1/T per second, looped with CYCLE_LOOP and frequency 1.

## Key findings (short)

1. **v is the downstream coordinate in every waterfall/rapids mesh.** For all three bodies (Inner/Foam/CrossStream), 100% of the triangle area has dP/dv pointing downstream: the -Y component on the lip and river section, -Z on the drop. The model's downstream direction is **-Y then down**. u runs across the fall (+u ≈ +X; corr(u, x) = 0.89 for Tall and 0.91 for Slope). Every scroll controller **decreases** V Offset, so the textures move toward +v, i.e. downstream. The only +V-Offset meshes are the mist "FillMesh"/"jetPuffs" cards, which move upward.
2. **Scroll speeds (UV offset units per second; mesh-v/s = rate / UVScale.v):**
    - Tall Inner (Lighting): V -0.12/s (T=8.333 s) => 0.12 v/s.
    - Tall Foam (Effect, UVScale 2,3): V -0.3125/s (T=3.2) => 0.104 v/s.
    - Tall CrossStream (UVScale 2,1.5): V -0.333/s (T=3.0) => 0.222 v/s.
    - Slope Inner: V -0.1714/s (T=5.833) => 0.171 v/s.
    - Slope Foam (2,3): V -0.4286/s (T=2.333) => 0.143 v/s.
    - Slope CrossStream (1,1): V -0.4286/s => 0.429 v/s.
    - Lighting bodies also have small looping U Offset wobbles (Tall ±0.036, Slope -0.071..+0.092) and U/V Scale wobbles (1.00..1.03/1.05 and 0.989..1.0). These have no net drift.
    - Thin falls: V -0.76 to -0.857/s plus U Offset +0.03/s, with a U Scale wobble. Skirt fog: V -0.5455/s. Rapids: V -0.075 to -0.6/s.
3. **The UV density is strongly non-uniform, and this encodes acceleration.** World units per 1.0 v on Tall Inner, by z quintile from bottom to top: 3944, 3395, 793, 1060, 1245. On Slope Inner: 3729, 1995, 1896, 2228, 2049. At a constant UV scroll, the apparent world speed is therefore about 3-5x higher on the vertical drop than at the lip. Tall Inner runs at ~95-150 u/s on the lip and ~410-475 u/s on the drop. Tall Foam runs at ~80-130 u/s on the lip and ~355-410 u/s on the drop. Slope Inner runs at ~325-385 u/s on the upper part and ~640 u/s at the bottom.
4. **Vertex colors:**
    - The Lighting bodies have alpha = 1 everywhere. Their RGB is a dark blue-green tint (R 0.03-0.41, G 0.06-0.55, B 0.05-0.53) that gets brighter downward: corr(lum, z) = -0.65 (Tall) and -0.53 (Slope).
    - Tall Foam: RGB is grey 0-1 and luminance rises downward (corr -0.67). Alpha ranges 0-0.93 (mean 0.63) and **follows v**: corr(A, v) = +0.88, with v-quintile means 0.23, 0.58, 0.71, 0.77, 0.88. This is a top-to-bottom fade-in, not an edge fade: border 0.58 vs interior 0.65, and only the u-extreme border vertices are low (0.35).
    - Slope Foam: alpha is an **edge fade**. Border vertices average 0.008 and interior vertices 0.83 (49/180 vertices are exactly 0), with corr(A, distance to border) = 0.69 and no v dependence (corr -0.03).
    - CrossStream ribbons: alpha is binary-ish. All open-border vertices are 0 and interior vertices are 0.75-1.0 (Tall: 304 at 0, 55 at ~0.75, 55 at 1.0), i.e. a feathered ribbon edge.
    - No mesh has a channel that looks like a speed or flow-vector encoding.
5. **CurrentPlane:** 12 in Tall, 13 in Slope, 19 in Tall02 and 1 in each Thin 2048/3096/4096. Each is a hidden-material (no shader) 4-vertex quad with ±125 to ±175 local half-size, under a `NiNode "CurrentPlane"`. The `CurrentPlane` string exists in SkyrimSE.exe, next to `WaterCurrentGeometryNode` and the console command `ToggleWaterCurrentGeometry`, so the engine looks these nodes up. Every node is Rz(180°)·Rx(tilt). The **node's local +Y axis is the downstream direction in model space**: it lies within 2-28° of the surface +v on Slope. On Tall, the planes are tilted only 0-32°, while the drop is near-vertical, so they deviate 46-79° there. They are laid out as a 2-3 column grid along the fall. They only appear in files that have WATER-material havok collision (BSXFlags 643). That they drive the water-current system is an inference (**uncertain**).
6. **Emitters:**
    - Slope `FXWaterfallBodySlopeMeshEmit:0` (flags 0xF = AppCulled) is 60 disjoint triangles. Their vertex colors are black with alpha 1, the mean normal is (0, -0.80, 0.60), and they sit 33-87 units off the foam surface. Two NiPSysMeshEmitters use them with EMIT_FROM_FACE_SURFACE and VELOCITY_USE_NORMALS at speed 150, planar gravity 315 (Gravity18 axis (0, -0.68, -0.73)), drag 5%, and two planar deflectors.
    - Tall/Tall02 have a NiPSysBoxEmitter at the lip: 586 x 33.6 x 16 at (-12.7, -37, 25.2), emitting along (0, -1, -0.04), i.e. downstream, at speed 210±105 (Tall02: 201). Planar gravity is 135/144 along (0, 0.156, -0.988).
