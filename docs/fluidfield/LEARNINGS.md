# Fluid Field — Learnings

Engine quirks, tooling surprises and repo conventions discovered while building
Fluid Field. Add an entry whenever something cost time to figure out, so the
next task starts from it. Newest entries go at the bottom of each section.

Entry format: **Short title** (date, phase) — what happens, where it was seen
(file paths / line ranges, RenderDoc capture, Ghidra address), and what to do
about it.

## Engine and rendering

-   **Two features keep engine shadow textures across frames** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Skylighting (`src/Features/Skylighting.cpp:660-668`) and Exponential Height Fog both keep a reference to an engine shadow-map texture from one frame to the next. Fluid Field must not copy this; see `reports/skylighting.md` and `reports/volumetric-lighting.md`.
-   **Unified Water turns off vanilla actor wakes** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). It disables the vanilla dynamic flowmap writes in `Main::RenderWaterEffects`, so `ISWaterFlow` wakes no longer appear. Water also has no foam term today. See `reports/unified-water.md`.
-   **Hooked call sites are shared** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Skylighting and Upscaling both hook the precipitation-occlusion call (`RelocationID(35560, 36559)`). Grass Optimizations and Grass Collision both hook `BSGrassShader::SetupGeometry`. Check what is already chained before adding a hook.
-   **`Util::GetShapeBound` never skips list shapes** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). `src/Utils/ActorUtils.cpp:14` casts the rigid body to `hkpListShape` instead of its shape, so the check never fires. It also reduces every Havok shape to a sphere. This is an existing bug, not ours; report it upstream rather than fixing it in this branch.
-   **Skylighting replaces the vanilla precipitation-occlusion call** (2026-10-13, R1). With the shader cache on, the vanilla function at `RelocationID(35560, 36559) + 0x3A1` is never called. Skylighting runs `Precipitation::SetupMask`/`RenderMask` itself (`src/Features/Skylighting.cpp:523-560`). The pass is depth-only: `BSUtilityShader` `RenderDepth` in render mode `kPrecipitationOcclusionMap = 0x1C`. Its depth target's format, size and near/far planes are not in source; reading them needs RenderDoc.
-   **Landscape heights are readable on the CPU** (2026-10-13, R1). `TESObjectLAND::loadedData->heights[4][289]` holds 4 quadrants of 17x17 vertices, so 33x33 per cell at 128-unit spacing. Vertices are row-major `y*17+x`; quadrant bit 0 is +X and bit 1 is +Y (`src/EngineFixes/LandscapeSeamsFix.cpp:45-48,555-558`). The vertices continue under water, so they give a riverbed. They are **not** absolute world Z: add `heightExtents.x - min(heights)` per cell to get world heights, which then match `TES::GetLandHeight` exactly at vertices (verified 2026-10-14 over 60 cells in two worldspaces, `reports/r1-stage-a-checks.md`). Between vertices, bilinear sampling differs from the engine's triangle interpolation by up to about 49 units on steep cells.
-   **Vanilla water surfaces are in `TESWaterSystem::waterObjects`** (2026-10-13, R1). Each object has an `NiPlane` and `multiBounds` AABBs (`extern/CommonLibSSE-NG/include/RE/T/TESWaterObject.h:27-36`). Unified Water removes its LOD tiles from the list, so it holds only near water. `src/Features/Skin.cpp:294-329` already walks it. `GetExteriorWaterHeight()` returns -inf for interiors.
-   **Wetness Effects has a dead binding** (2026-10-13, R1). It binds the engine rain-occlusion SRV at PS t70 every frame (`src/Features/WetnessEffects.cpp:947-954`), but no shader samples `TexPrecipOcclusion`.
-   **ImGui frames are skipped unless something asks for them** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). `OverlayRenderer::ShouldSkipRendering` (`src/Menu/OverlayRenderer.cpp`) skips the whole ImGui frame unless the menu, the editor, the performance overlay or a few other things are active, so an `OverlayFeature::DrawOverlay` never runs during play. Fluid Field adds itself to that list; any new always-on overlay must do the same.
-   **D3D11 silently nulls an SRV whose resource is still bound as a UAV** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). Ping-pong compute passes must unbind the previous pass's UAV before binding its texture as an SRV. `WindSolver::Dispatch` does this for every pass.
-   **Water planes cover whole cells, and waterfalls are not water** (2026-10-14, R1 stage b). Water objects and cell water are flat planes over a whole bound or cell, regardless of terrain, so "a plane exists here" is not "it is wet here": compare with the landscape bed. Waterfalls never appear among `TESWaterSystem::waterObjects` (no sloped bounds met on the Riverwood–Whiterun route); they are effect meshes and particles, so treat them as flow sources found from references. `reports/r1-stage-b-play-session.md`.
-   **Landscape collision is layer 17 (`kGround`), not 13 (`kTerrain`)** (2026-10-14, R1 route run). Downward rays over land hit `kGround` with no reference; placed meshes hit `kStatic` (1) with one. Layer 13 never appeared in 1768 logged hits. `reports/r1-route-run.md` section 4.
-   **A ray from above the water hits low bridge and pier decks first** (2026-10-14, R1 route run). Telling a deck (air underneath) from a rock rising out of the water takes a second, upward ray from just under the surface: it hits a deck's underside, but nothing (or only the top) inside a solid. Fish, plants, lumber piles and horses are on non-static layers and are stepped through.
-   **A thin deck reads like the inside of a solid to height-only probes** (2026-10-14, water slice 1). An upward ray under a plank or walkway hits its underside at almost the top's height, the same as a ray from inside a rock hitting the rock's top. Judging by the hit's normal (pointing down for an underside) is the next attempt; whether Havok reports normals facing the ray is unverified. Seen on reference `0002B8E5` near (27650, -34600). The mesh cut (D14) replaces this decision.
-   **Static meshes keep their geometry on the CPU** (2026-10-14, D15). `BSGraphics::TriShape` (`rendererData`, BSGeometry +0x138) holds `rawVertexData` (+0x20) and `rawIndexData` (+0x28) for every dock, pier, wall, bridge and boat checked (the Riverwood bridge, 160 references at the Riften canal and 602 at the lake docks). Only tree shapes lacked it. Vertex positions are 4 floats in SE/AE even when `VF_FULLPREC` is not set (`vertexDesc` flags `0x3B`, stride 32); use `VertexDesc::GetSize` and `GetAttributeOffset(VA_POSITION)`. Checked by reading the running game's memory, read-only (`skyrim-engine-re/tools/live/`).
-   **The normal-based probe gets arches right and pillars wrong** (2026-10-14). Under the Riverwood stone bridge (`00022469`), upward probes report downward normals (z -0.44 to -1.00) that follow the arch curve, so arches classed open. Not one point along the bridge classed solid, so the water ran through both pillars. Superseded by the mesh cut (D15).
-   **Whether water flows is a property of its water type, not of the flowmap** (2026-10-14). Unified Water's world flowmap has strong flow vectors under Lake Ilinalta too (average strength 0.74, like the Riverwood rapids), and none under the Sea of Ghosts at Dawnstar. Vanilla keeps lakes still because their water type (`TESWaterForm`) has no `kEnableFlowmap` flag; each type also carries `data.flowmapScale`, `linearVelocity` and its own wave settings. Read the type from `TESWaterObject::waterType`. The flowmap decodes as RG \* 2 - 1 = flow in world axes and A = intensity; B is 255 along rivers, so it is not a strength.
-   **Work queued by distance must not be dropped when the player is far** (2026-10-14, water slice 1). Bed rays are cached, so a refinement square dropped while out of range was never queued again, and the Riverwood bridge was never refined. Out-of-range work now waits in a deferred list.

## Resource lifetimes and loading screens

-   **Few features release GPU resources** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Grass Collision and Water Effects create their resources once and never free them. Skylighting and Screen Space GI queue a reset when a loading screen closes, and that is the pattern to copy. Grass Collision also keeps stale state in function statics and only updates on frames that draw grass, so it stops updating in interiors.

## Threading

-   **`FluidField::Prepass` runs on the main thread** (2026-10-14, R1 stage a). Every `[Fluid Field] Landscape check` log line carries the same thread ID as the plugin-load lines (`Loading Feature`), so reading the engine's cell grid and calling `TES::GetLandHeight` from Prepass is on the main thread. The log's second column is the thread ID, which makes this easy to re-check.
-   **Locks to take when reading engine data** (2026-10-13, R1): `TESWaterSystem::lock` (a spinlock) while iterating `waterObjects`; on 1.7.99+, the terrain-cell lock `REL::ID(564236)` around `gridCells->GetCell` (as `src/Features/UnifiedWater.cpp:593-603` does); `bhkWorld::worldLock` around raw `hkpWorld::CastRay`. Whether our hook context is safe for Havok queries is UNVERIFIED (R1 open question 2).

## Build, packaging and deployment

-   **Driving tests through devbench** (2026-10-14, R1 route run). Fast-travel only to persistent references (map markers; find them with `inspect kind=refs model=marker_map`). Dismount (`Actor.Dismount` on the player) before any `coc`, or the camera stays with the horse. A spawned horse follows the camera, so set the camera direction before galloping. `communityshaders.capture kind=screenshot` keeps the Fluid Field overlay in the screenshot; devbench's native capture does not. Screenshots land in the game's `Screenshots` folder.
-   **Committing while a build runs breaks the next deploy** (2026-10-14). A new git HEAD makes the build delete `build/ALL/aio`, and two parallel steps then repopulate it and race on the same files ("Error copying file ... No such file or directory"). Fix: `cmake --build --preset=Deploy --target PREPARE_AIO` on its own, then the normal deploy build. Better: do not commit while a build runs.
-   **Any feature's version bump discards the engine shader cache index** (2026-10-14). `ShaderCache::ValidateDiskCache` asks every feature's `ValidateCache`, which fails when the `.ini` version differs from `Data\ShaderCache\Info.ini`, so bumping Fluid Field 0-4-0 to 0-5-0 deleted the disk cache (stored blobs are kept, so the recompile is partial). Fluid Field adds no engine shader defines, so it overrides `ValidateCache`/`WriteDiskCacheInfo` to opt out; remove that once it adds `FLUID_FIELD` to `Water.hlsl`. The log line to look for is `Disk cache outdated: feature validation failed`.
-   **devbench reports a running game as gone during long blocking scenarios** (2026-10-14). A scenario that blocks for about 30 s or more returns `game not running (target: SE)` even though the game is fine. Run long scenarios with `async: true` and poll `action: status`, and check `tasklist` before calling it a crash. `communityshaders.feature set` replaces the whole settings blob, so read the settings first, pass every field and restore them afterwards.
-   **CMake and vcpkg come from VS 18** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Neither was on PATH originally. The user PATH now includes VS's bundled CMake (4.3.1, `Common7\IDE\CommonExtensions\Microsoft\CMake\CMake\bin`), and the user variable `VCPKG_ROOT` points to `C:\Program Files\Microsoft Visual Studio\18\Community\VC\vcpkg`, the copy that originally configured `build/ALL`. Terminals opened before that change need restarting. `BuildRelease.bat` loads vcvars itself for the Ninja preset.
-   **cmd will not run `.bat` files from the current directory** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). From a non-interactive shell, `BuildRelease.bat` fails with "not recognized" (NoDefaultCurrentDirectoryInExePath). Call it by its absolute path. `CMakeLists.txt` already works around the same issue for its robocopy wrapper.
-   **The ALL preset forces `AUTO_PLUGIN_DEPLOYMENT=OFF`** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Deployment uses the local `CMakeUserPresets.json`: configure preset `ALL-WITH-AUTO-DEPLOYMENT` (shares `build/ALL`) and build preset `Deploy`, run as `BuildRelease.bat Deploy ALL-WITH-AUTO-DEPLOYMENT`. A plain `BuildRelease.bat ALL` reconfigures `build/ALL` with deployment off again. The first deploy after a commit redeploys everything, because CMake detects the new git HEAD.
-   **Dev toolchain setup on this machine** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). Python 3.13 is installed per user (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Python\Python313`, on the user PATH), with `pre-commit` and `hlslkit` installed through pip. `pre-commit install` has added the git hook, so clang-format, prettier and gersemi run on every commit. `tools/extract-i18n.py --check`, `--orphans` and `sort-i18n.py --check` all pass with the hand-added Fluid Field keys.
-   **A non-core feature is packaged from its folder alone** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). CMake creates `dist/<Folder Name>-<timestamp>.zip` for every `features/<Folder>/` without a `CORE` file, and globs `src/` automatically. No CMake edits were needed.
-   **Integer modulus is a warning in fxc, and CI allows none** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). `int % int` raises X3556. Keep grid sizes powers of two and wrap with `a & (n - 1)`, which is also correct for negative cells.
-   **A standalone test can use the Windows SDK's C++/WinRT** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). With the Visual Studio generator the SDK `cppwinrt` headers are on the include path, and `winrt::check_hresult` only needs `windowsapp.lib`. `cmake -S tests/FluidField -B build/FluidFieldTest` then builds without vcpkg in a few seconds.
-   **A failed pre-commit hook can merge the next commit** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). If clang-format rejects a commit, its files stay staged and are swept into the next `git commit`. Run `pre-commit run --files <files>` before staging.

-   **Include-only shader changes are not recompiled** (2026-10-14). The shader disk cache is fresh while it is newer than the shader's own source file (`src/ShaderCache.cpp`, around line 1640); included files are not checked. The deploy copies only files whose contents changed, so `Water.hlsl` keeps its old time when only `FluidField/WaterNormal.hlsli` changes. After such a change, touch the **deployed** `Data/Shaders/Water.hlsl` (touching the repository copy is not enough), or bump the feature version.
-   **Offline HLSL checks with fxc** (2026-10-14). `fxc.exe` from the Windows SDK compiles a shader permutation in seconds: `fxc /T ps_5_0 /E main /I package/Shaders /I "features/<Name>/Shaders" ... /D PSHADER /D SPECULAR ... package/Shaders/Water.hlsl`. In Git Bash, set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` first, or `/T` is rewritten as a path. Water pixel shaders need `SPECULAR` (or `UNDERWATER`) for `HPosition` to exist.

-   **Water's `dirColor + ambientColor` is the water's colour, not the light** (2026-10-14). In `Water.hlsl`, `ExtractLighting` splits `refractionDiffuseColor` (the shallow/deep water colour, already lit) into a directional and an ambient part. Anything that should look white under the scene light (foam) must use `ShadowSampling::GetDirectionalLighting()` and `GetAmbientLighting()` instead; with the extracted parts, foam came out dark green-grey.
-   **Steeper water normals turn grazing views black** (2026-10-14). Raising vanilla's ripple amplitude (up to 2.5x on rough simulated water) tipped reflections below the horizon at low view angles, where the water reflects the dark lower half of the sky. A live A/B with Surface Normal and Flow Detail at 0 stayed dark, which pointed at the amplitude boost. Keep reflections at least half as high as flat water would give them, and fade any boost out toward the horizon.
-   **Vanilla effect meshes can carry real geometry** (2026-10-14). `FXrapidsRocks01` holds lit rocks beside its foam. Hide only the leaves that are not drawn with `BSLightingShaderProperty`, not the reference's root.

-   **Vanilla's flowmap normals cannot be steered per frame** (2026-10-14). `GetFlowmapNormal` offsets its lookup by the game time times the flowmap's alpha, along the flowmap's direction, and rotates the whole coordinate by that direction. It is stable only because the flowmap never changes. Feeding it a changing direction or speed shifts the texture by time times the change, which is large after a while in game, and the water flickers. Blending the flow normals out (a weight) is safe.
-   **A contrast-preserving two-phase blend needs the pattern's true mean** (2026-10-14). Dividing by sqrt(w^2 + (1 - w)^2) around an assumed mean of 0.5 made the foam pattern (lengths of normal-map xy, mean well below 0.5) swell mid-fade and pulse harder. It is right for normal-map xy, whose mean is 0.

-   **The engine's per-frame camera buffer is only trustworthy during the deferred passes** (2026-10-15). A compute pass drawn after `Main_RenderWorld` (after water and transparents) that bound `*globals::game::perFrame` at b12 flickered: by then the buffer can hold another view's camera on some frames. Copy it into your own constant buffer during `Deferred::DeferredPasses` and bind that copy later. Also bind b5/b6 (SharedData, FeatureData) yourself; other passes unbind them.
-   **Thin layers need fine samples where they are** (2026-10-15). Marching an 18-unit fog layer with steps of a hundred units hits or misses it per frame. March each half of the ray from its own end with geometrically growing steps (4 units first), and clip to the layer's band using the ground at the ray's ends, not the highest ground around.

-   **Vanilla's effect palette overwrites colour set earlier in `Effect.hlsl`** (2026-10-15). With GrayscaleToColor, `baseColor.xyz` is replaced by the palette lookup well after the base texture is sampled, and GrayscaleToAlpha recomputes alpha the same way. Colour or alpha changes made right after the base sample vanish; make them after the palette block. Debug colours belong on `psout.Diffuse`.
-   **The effect vertex shader cannot use `FrameBuffer`** (2026-10-15): b12 is taken there ("cbuffer bank 12 used more than once"). Use `PerGeometry`'s `PosAdjust` (world = camera-relative + PosAdjust); the pixel shader can use `FrameBuffer::CameraPosAdjust`.
-   **Waterfall rock capture by Havok rays is sound** (2026-10-15). At the White River falls the hits are mostly static (layer 1), ground (17) and terrain (13); boulders come out sharp in the dumped heightfields (`FluidField_rock_site*.bin` beside the log; `rock_view.py`-style hillshade). When an effect built on it does not show, suspect the shader path first.
-   **Warping a waterfall's texture cannot make water part around rock** (2026-10-15). Shifting vanilla's streaks (constant u) by a push field bends them a little; with foam collars and a thinned wake it still reads as water cutting through the boulder, and pushing the coarse mesh's vertices far tears its triangles. Parting needs a real flow on the surface (a solver with the rock as walls).
-   **Deploying right after killing the game can hit a locked DLL** (2026-10-15): MSB8066 on the plugin stamp. Wait a few seconds and deploy again.

-   **Vanilla's falling-water models are a lit surface under effect overlays** (2026-10-15). `FXWaterfallBody*.nif` holds an inner surface drawn by the lighting shader (BSLightingShaderProperty, no effect texture), a white-water sheet (BSEffectShader, FXWhiteWater01), streak layers (FXfluidTile01) and spray particle systems (NiParticleSystem). The water you see is mostly the inner surface; the effect layers read brown and ragged on their own. Changing only the effect shader touches the overlays. `FXWaterfallThin*.nif` has no lit surface: only streak layers and particles.

-   **Lit geometry must keep its exact vertex position** (2026-10-15). BSLightingShader surfaces are drawn after a depth prepass and the colour pass depth-tests against it for equality. Recomputing the clip position in Lighting.hlsl, even mathematically the same (`mul(ViewProj, worldPosition)` instead of `mul(modelView, inputPosition)`), changed the depth in the last bits and the surface drew as a flat brown. Displace lit geometry only in a pass that also displaces its prepass, or not at all; change its look in the pixel shader.

-   **Superseded (D56): vanilla's falling-water texture runs along u** (2026-10-16): on `FXWaterfallBodyTall` u spans 0.08..3.39 against v -2.04..-0.97. Laying a flow's along coordinate on v makes herringbone across the streaks. Wrong: the NIF dissection (research/waterfalls/vanilla-nif-dissection.md) shows v increases downstream over all of every falls mesh, and vanilla scrolls toward +v.
-   **The ambient SH's directional terms read warm under waterfalls** (2026-10-16): `SharedData::GetAmbient(normal)` came out tan there and made custom water brown. The DC term (`AmbientSHR.x` etc. times 0.886) with the material's own tint matched vanilla's falls; check colours by measuring captures against vanilla in the same view, not by eye.

## Repository conventions

-   **There is no `AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). `AGENTS.md` fills that role and points to `.claude/CLAUDE.md`. `.github/copilot-instructions.md` still mentions `AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`; that is upstream text and we left it alone.
-   **VR support was removed from Community Shaders** in `c9d7c0524` (2026-06-07, #2475), but `CMakePresets.json` still sets `ENABLE_SKYRIM_VR=ON` in the ALL preset; `CMakeLists.txt` no longer reads it. The roadmap's VR compatibility row and spike R9 need revisiting.
-   **Snow Deformation is not in this repo** (2026-10-13, Phase 0). It lives in open upstream PR #2751 and the fork `josefplyr1/skyrim-community-shaders-dynamic-snow-deformation`. Its Havok input is the same sphere bound that Grass Collision uses, and it reads no velocities, masses or contacts. See `reports/snow-deformation.md`.
-   **The root `CMakeLists.txt` is not gersemi-clean on `dev`** (2026-10-13, Phase 1). Any edit makes the hook reformat about 90 unrelated lines. Keep Fluid Field build logic out of it (the solver test is a standalone project for this reason).

## Vanilla water planes reach us as whole cells (2026-10-16)

The water planes paired with a waterfall site (WaterSurfaces planes, water objects and cell water
alike) carry bounds of a whole 4096-unit cell, far wider than their water. Treating "below a
plane's height inside its bounds" as "under that pool" swallowed most of a waterfall. A pool's
water has to be confirmed from the captured rock (bed under the level, and the falls' surface at
the level or the bed shallow under it). (D40)

## Utility shaders got no feature defines; lit draws have a depth pre-pass (2026-10-16)

The lit falls (BSLightingShader) are drawn after a depth pre-pass by BSUtilityShader. Anything that
removes lit pixels (discard, clip) must be mirrored in Utility.hlsl, or the pre-pass's depth stays
and the G-buffer shows blank there (milky, then tan). ShaderCache's Utility define builder did not
add feature defines at all until D40, so a feature's #if in Utility.hlsl was dead code. Also: CS
compiles shader permutations asynchronously, so the first seconds after a load or a settings change
render with older shaders; wait 5 to 10 s before judging a capture.

-   **The falls' lit surface has no NiAlphaProperty** (2026-10-17, D53). `FXWaterfallBodyTallInner01` and `FXWaterfallBodySlopeMesh01` (BSLightingShaderProperty, flags `0000802182400381`: no alpha bits) carry no alpha property, so they draw opaque with a depth pre-pass. The effect layers of the same models (`FXWaterfallBodySlopeFoam`, `L2_whiteCrossStream04`, `PCloudSplash*`) carry alpha flags `0x10ED`: blending on, SrcAlpha / InvSrcAlpha, test off, threshold 128.

-   **Never compute a scroll as a measured rate times total time** (2026-10-17, D60). Vanilla's falls loop a slow V-scale controller (6.5 s), and frame hitches move any measured rate; `rate * Time` turns each change into a jump of change x session time (half a repeat after five minutes): the texture runs back and forth, worse the longer the session. Integrate the phase once a frame instead.
-   **Havok ray hits do not say "from behind"** (2026-10-17, D57). The hit normal's facing never told a ray starting inside rock from one outside it; ray parity (count the crossings from a point known to be outside) does. A sheet before a cliff has the cliff on one side only: require rock on both sides of the sheet for "inside" (D59).
-   **The falls' own collision is water material on the static layer** (2026-10-17, D56). Downward rays for the rock capture hit it under every fall (4622 of 32786 rays) until it was skipped by model path (fxwaterfall, fxrapids).
-   **`near` and `far` are Windows macros** (2026-10-17). A local named `near` fails to compile in any file that pulls in windows.h; name it something else.
-   **Judge flow in motion, not in stills** (2026-10-17, D60). A warp that looks right in a still and in the debug view still read as vanilla's motion to the user. To measure motion: `sgtm 0.1`, consecutive captures with `allowTimeScale`, phase correlation on a water-only patch.
-   **The engine builds a BSTriShape from plain arrays** (2026-10-18, D62, runtime-proven on 1.7.104). `77465` (BSShaderResourceManager slot 01) takes triangle count, u16 indices, vertex count, positions, uv0, (uv1), normals, tangents, bitangents, colours, and skin, land and eye arrays (null arrays left out of the layout). It returns an unreferenced shape that owns its renderer data. Give it a property (a cloned lit property, then SetupGeometry and FinishSetupGeometry) and attach it under any loaded node. Never point `rawVertexData`/`rawIndexData` at our own memory: the engine frees them with MemoryManager. Research: skyrim-engine-re kb evidence 2026-10-18-runtime-geometry.
-   **Falls' lit vertex layouts read VF_FULLPREC false** (2026-10-18, D61) although their positions are four floats (UV at offset 16). Infer full precision from the attribute offsets, not the flag.
-   **Engine water objects are whole-cell rectangles** (2026-10-19, D63). The upper pool of the test falls is x 28672..32768, y -36864..-32768: its edge is the cell border, not the lip. Where the water visibly ends has to come from the rock.
-   **Parity from far above is not a reliable inside-rock test near falls** (2026-10-19, D63). Rock in front of the fall, overhangs and one-sided collision all add crossings. Use rays that start near the point and go outward, or the top-down capture where the ground is open.
-   **Plot the data, not just screenshots** (2026-10-18). The sheet dumps (FluidField_sheet\*.bin) plotted over the captured rock (scratchpad sheetplot.py) showed at once that columns started inside rock, slid under the pools and crossed each other. Screenshots alone had hidden that for several rounds.
-   **`FAR` is a Windows macro too** (2026-10-20, D80), like `near` and `far`: a constant named `FAR` compiled in a standalone harness and failed in the plugin's PCH build.
-   **A new .cpp needs a CMake re-configure** (2026-10-20). With the Visual Studio generator, `cmake --build` did not re-glob and the build failed with an unresolved symbol; re-configure with `cmake --preset ALL-WITH-AUTO-DEPLOYMENT`. A bare `cmake -S . -B build/ALL` drops the preset's game folder (CommunityShadersOutputDir), and Deploy then silently copies nowhere: the game kept the old DLL.
-   **Retiming a falls texture by physics stretches it** (2026-10-20, D80). Vanilla's falls UVs already speed up down the drops (rows are 3-28 world units apart). A physical speed against vanilla's scroll sat at the clamp on most of each mesh, a 2x squeeze. Keep vanilla's rate down the flow and let the sim bend only across it.
-   **Build the sim's code into an offline harness** (2026-10-20, D80). FallStrands.cpp has no engine types, so scratchpad strand/strandbake.cpp compiles it unchanged and runs it on the bake dumps in about 10 ms each. Stretch and back-flow numbers on all 18 dumps take seconds, not a game round.
-   **Some water effect models carry real rock** (2026-10-20, D81). FXrapidsRocks01.nif and FXCreekFlatLong01.nif hold lit boulder meshes (BSLightingShaderProperty, mountain slab texture) beside their effect layers; hide effect models per shape, never by root. The scratchpad bsanif.py lists a vanilla nif's parts from the BSA.
-   **The landscape height grid is no rock test for falls** (2026-10-20, D82). Its points are 128 units apart, so it smooths every cliff, and a curtain falling down a cliff lies under the interpolated surface. A "below the ground" test called a third of the visible falls rock.
-   **Small rock changes move the whole strand layout** (2026-10-20, D82). Flipping 2% of a mesh's texels between rock and water moved the streaks of 31% of its visible water by more than a texel (strands choose sides early and carry the choice down). Treat the rock mask as part of the look: change it only with the user judging.
-   **Touching the big shaders every launch cost 2-3 minutes a boot** (2026-10-20). The scratchpad launch script touched Lighting.hlsl, Utility.hlsl and Effect.hlsl so Community Shaders would recompile after a change to an include (its disk cache does not see includes). It now touches them only when a hash of the deployed .hlsl and .hlsli files changed since the last launch: boot to world 17 s, was about 160 s.
-   **Judge in-game shots only after the log says the site's flow is linked** (2026-10-20). A screenshot taken while a site was still baking showed vanilla's mesh, and its humps were read as the new swell. Check `Waterfall flow linked` (and the swell line) for that site first.
-   **Anything the vertex shaders read per frame must be latched per rendered frame** (2026-10-20, D85). The falls' depth pre-pass draws before FluidField::Prepass, the lit pass after; a value Prepass advances (the scroll phase) differs between them. Feeding it into vertex positions failed the lit pass's depth test almost everywhere (flat background, the falls only in cracks). Latch on `globals::state->frameCount` at the first draw of the frame.
-   **Bumping a feature .ini also loses the deploy folder** (2026-10-20). The .ini is globbed with CONFIGURE_DEPENDS, so a version bump makes `cmake --build --preset=Deploy` reconfigure without the preset's CommunityShadersOutputDir environment, and Deploy reports "Full deployment" while copying nothing. After an .ini change, run `cmake --preset ALL-WITH-AUTO-DEPLOYMENT` before deploying, and check the game folder's DLL time.
